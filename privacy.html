Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how Stonespace handles information through stonespacebrowser.com and the Stonespace Browser macOS application.

Information you provide

The website registration and support forms may collect your name, email address, phone number, occupation, school where applicable, support category, and message. Registration and support submissions are stored using Google services.

Purchases and licenses

Lemon Squeezy processes checkout, payment, subscription, receipt, and license information. Stonespace does not directly receive your full payment-card number. When you activate the app, the license key and an installation name containing your Mac's computer name are sent to Lemon Squeezy for activation and validation.

Information stored on your Mac

The app stores settings and bookmarks locally. It stores the license key using macOS secure encryption together with license status information. Stonespace does not automatically receive your browsing history, bookmarks, or screenshots.

Screen recording permission

The app requests macOS Screen Recording permission only for screenshot features you choose to use. Screenshots are saved or copied on your Mac and are not automatically uploaded to Stonespace.

Websites you visit

Websites opened in Stonespace Browser receive information as they would when opened in another browser and are governed by their own privacy policies.

How information is used

  • To provide purchases, subscriptions, downloads, and license activation.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To operate and improve the product and website.
  • To prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

Information is shared with service providers such as Lemon Squeezy and Google only as needed for the purposes described above. Stonespace does not sell personal information.

Choices and contact

You may request access to or deletion of information held by Stonespace by emailing info.stonespacebrowser@gmail.com. Some purchase or tax records may need to be retained as required by law.

California

Stonespace is based in California. California residents may contact us regarding applicable privacy rights.
